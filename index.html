What is CYE

What is CYE?

The CYE Competition — short for Creative Young Entrepreneur — is a pitch competition dedicated to fostering innovation, creativity, and entrepreneurial spirit among today's brightest minds. It's a platform where aspiring founders showcase their groundbreaking businesses, pitch them live, and compete for a chance to turn their dreams into reality.

Our vision

VISION

Our vision is simple yet profound: to empower the next generation of entrepreneurs to transform their businesses and drive positive change in the world. We believe entrepreneurship is the driving force behind innovation, economic growth, and social progress. Through the CYE Competition, we aim to identify, nurture, and support the most promising entrepreneurs on their journey to success.

Every entry came down to three things — kept light, because if you run a real business, you're closer than you think.

A concise deck introducing your active business — upload a PDF or slides, or paste a Google Drive link.

PDF / slides / Drive link · Required

Max 2 min · Drive link

Guidelines

The rules, kept simple.

A few requirements that keep CYE fair and impactful for everyone.

Accuracy & Honesty

All profiles, pitches and presentations must accurately represent your venture. Any misrepresentation leads to disqualification.

Clear Differentiation

Your deck should clearly set your business apart from competitors — clarity and detail strongly improve your chances of becoming a finalist.

One Representative

Only one representative per business may take part. A single person must be the official contestant and presenter through all stages.

Submission Deadline

Applications closed on 31 August 2026, in line with the deadline set by JCI World Headquarters. Late applications are not accepted.

English Only

All forms and presentations must be in English — also the language used for pitching and training sessions.

One Submission

Only one submission per business is allowed, keeping the competition fair for everyone.
