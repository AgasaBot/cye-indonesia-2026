Creative Young Entrepreneur Award

How to join — Indonesia 2026

Pitch your business → win a fully-funded trip to the 2027 JCI ASPAC in Taiwan (flights, hotel & cash).

📅 Deadline 31 Aug 2026 💵 IDR 200k early-bird 📍 Final 3 Oct · Jakarta

✅ Who can join

  • Aged 18–40
  • Running a business that's been active 3+ months
  • Open to all — you do not need to be a JCI member
  • Application & pitching are in English

📝 How to enter ~3 min, online

  1. Register at cye-indonesia.com
  2. Pay the fee — IDR 200,000 (early-bird, through 14 Aug) / IDR 300,000 from 15 Aug
  3. Submit your 3 materials (below)

⏰ Deadline: 31 August 2026 — late entries won't be considered.

📎 What to submit

  1. Business Introduction Deck — a concise deck about your active business (PDF, slides, or Google Drive link).
  2. Pitch Video — max 2 minutes (Google Drive or YouTube link).40s intro · 20s problem + SDG · 50s solution & impact · 10s closing
  3. Headshot — a high-quality photo of you, the founder.

🗓️ Key dates

  • 31 Aug 2026 — applications close
  • September — Top 30 shortlist → review & mentorship → Top 20 semi-finalists
  • 3 Oct 2026 — National Final, Jakarta (Top 20 → Top 4 → winner)

🏆 The prize

The national winner represents Indonesia at the 2027 JCI ASPAC in Taiwan — flights, hotel & cash covered — plus dedicated 1:1 mentoring to prepare for the stage.
